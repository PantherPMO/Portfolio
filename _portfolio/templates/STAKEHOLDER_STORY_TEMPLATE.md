## 6. The Five-Minute Presentation

**Write this out in full, as you would actually say it.** This is the interview rehearsal, and it is the most valuable section in the document.

---

### [0:00–0:30] The headline

> <Open with the answer, not the method. Stakeholders want the conclusion first — the method earns its place only after they care about the result.>

### [0:30–1:30] What we found

> <The two or three findings, each with its number and its mechanism.>

### [1:30–2:30] Why it is happening

> <The explanation. This is where you demonstrate that you understand the business, not only the data.>

### [2:30–3:30] What it is costing

> <The quantified implication. Make the number concrete and relatable.>

### [3:30–4:30] What I recommend

> <Specific actions, prioritised, with owners and expected impact.>

### [4:30–5:00] What I would watch, and what I am unsure about

> <Honest limitations, and the metrics that would tell them whether the actions are working. Ending on honesty builds more trust than ending on confidence.>
